VOICE CLEANER

Converts hotel YouTube scripts into narration-friendly text before TTS.

Examples:
4.2 -> 4 point 2
8.4/10 -> 8 point 4 out of 10
170 sq ft -> 170 square feet
11 min walk -> 11 minute walk
$22 -> 22 US dollars
INR 500 -> 500 Indian rupees
3-star -> three-star
WiFi -> Wi-Fi

Kaggle:
python voice_cleaner.py --input /kaggle/working/hotel_script.txt --output /kaggle/working/narration_ready.txt
