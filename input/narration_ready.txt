There's a hotel in San Diego where guests come back a second time, a third time, and in at least one case, a guest is already planning their fourth visit. It's not new. It's not flashy in the way a modern glass tower is. In fact, it's been standing in the same spot since the 1940s. But it holds a rating that's almost impossible to find in a city this size — 9.4 out of 10, based on nearly two thousand reviews. Today, we're taking a full walkthrough of the Lafayette Hotel and Club, and by the time we're done, you'll know exactly what to expect before you ever book a room.

A BIT OF HISTORY

Before we get into rooms and ratings, here's something that makes this stay feel different from a standard hotel visit. The Lafayette dates back to the 1940s, and it's long been known as one of San Diego's classic landmark hotels — the kind of property that Hollywood names and old San Diego society once passed through. That retro-glam identity isn't a modern design trend layered on top; it's baked into the building's actual history, and it's a big part of why the decor gets mentioned in nearly every single guest review.

Knowing that backstory changes how you experience the property. You're not just booking a room — you're stepping into a piece of San Diego's mid-century past that's been carefully kept alive.

FIRST IMPRESSIONS AND OVERALL RATING

Now, let's get into the numbers, because they set the tone for everything else. The Lafayette is rated a four-star property, and across one thousand nine hundred and forty-four guest reviews, it holds an overall score of 9.4 out of 10 — labeled "Exceptional" by Expedia's own rating system.

Break that down further and the picture gets even clearer. Cleanliness comes in at 9.6 out of 10. Staff friendliness scores 9.4. And service itself is rated as close to perfect as it gets. That's not a fluke across a handful of reviews — that's a pattern repeated by dozens of different travelers, describing dozens of different stays, all landing on the same conclusion.

One guest summed it up simply: "Absolutely beautiful and very clean. This is the only hotel I plan on staying at while in San Diego going forward." Another traveler, on their third stay at the property, said the beds were "the most comfortable ever" and that they were already looking forward to a fourth visit.

THE ROOMS, CATEGORY BY CATEGORY

Here's where you'll actually be sleeping, and the Lafayette offers more room variety than most travelers expect from a boutique-style property.

At the entry level, there's the Classic Room, available with either one king bed or two queen beds, comfortably sleeping two to four guests depending on the layout. This is the standard starting point for most bookings.

If you're traveling on a tighter budget, the Petite Room is worth a look — a smaller footprint, still with the same clean, well-kept standard the property is known for. There's even a Petite Room with Bunk Beds, a clever option for families traveling with kids or groups of friends splitting the cost.

Moving up the scale, there's a straightforward Room category offered with either one king bed or two queen beds, and a Poolside version of that same room with a private balcony — a nice touch if you want easy, direct access without extra stairs or hallways.

For travelers wanting more space, the Studio Suite with one king bed is a step up, while the full Suite category comes in two configurations: a one-king version that sleeps up to three guests, and a two-queen version that stretches to sleep up to five.

And then there's the standout of the entire property — the Family Suite. This is a genuine two-bedroom layout, built around a four-poster king bed, comfortably sleeping up to six guests, with a desk included. Pricing starts at around 169 dollars a night, with a total around 195 dollars once taxes and fees are factored in. For a two-bedroom suite that sleeps six people in a 9.4-rated property, that's a genuinely strong value.

Across nearly every room category, guests get a private bathroom stocked with shampoo, soap, and towels, plus free WiFi in every room and throughout the public areas — with in-room speeds rated at 25-plus Mbps, which is more than enough for streaming or getting work done. There's also a Smeg electric kettle in the room, a small detail that shows up by name in multiple guest reviews. One traveler wrote, quote, "the Smeg electric kettle was awesome," calling it out as a specific highlight right alongside the pool and the decor.

DINING AT THE PROPERTY

Food is clearly a strong point here, and it comes up unprompted in review after review. The property operates two full restaurants, plus a dedicated coffee shop and cafe on-site.

The in-house diner gets specific praise — one guest wrote that "breakfast at the diner was sooo good," while another highlighted the coffee and matcha specifically as standout items. A separate restaurant on the property, referenced by returning guests as Quixote, is mentioned as a dinner destination that keeps people coming back.

Daily breakfast service runs from 6 AM to noon, offered as a paid add-on rather than something automatically bundled into your room rate. Expect a range of roughly 7 to 38 dollars per person, whether you're booking for an adult or a child.

Room service is also available, so if you'd rather not leave your room at all during a lazy morning, that option is on the table too.

THE POOL AND ON-SITE FACILITIES

The pool is consistently the single most mentioned feature across guest reviews, and it's easy to see why. Multiple travelers describe it as the centerpiece of their entire stay. One guest called it, simply, "a must." Another said it was their favorite part of the whole property.

Here's an insider tip worth knowing before you go: the pool area tends to get the best natural light in the early-to-mid afternoon, which is exactly when it photographs best if you're planning to capture the retro architecture and palm-lined courtyard for your own content or memories. The building's mid-century arches and colorful tile work make it one of the more genuinely Instagram-worthy corners of the property — guests repeatedly describe taking photos "from every angle," especially around the upper suites and the courtyard area.

Beyond the pool, the Lafayette also has a full spa and a gym on-site, so travelers who want to keep up a workout routine or book a treatment don't need to leave the property to do either. There's also a game room mentioned by guests as a nice, low-key option for downtime, and a dedicated meeting room on-site for anyone blending work with the trip.

LOCATION, GETTING AROUND, AND NEARBY BEACHES

So where does all of this actually put you in San Diego? Balboa Park sits about a 19-minute walk away, very doable on foot or a short rideshare. Closer still, within roughly 12 to 18 minutes on foot, you'll find the San Diego Black Ensemble Theater, the Observatory North Park, and the Queen Bee's Art and Cultural Center — a solid cluster of cultural spots for an afternoon.

For quick food options right around the property, there's a McDonald's just two minutes away, a Starbucks and Taco Bell within about seven minutes, and Colima's Mexican Food and a Wendy's around the ten-minute mark.

If beaches are part of your plan, San Diego's coastline is easily reachable from here — Pacific Beach and Mission Beach are both roughly a 10 to 15 minute drive, making it realistic to spend your morning by the pool and your afternoon in the ocean without relocating hotels.

If you're arriving by air, San Diego International Airport is roughly a 17-minute drive. For getting around without a car, Mission Valley Center Station is about a 4-minute drive, and the Old Town Transit Center sits around 13 minutes away — both solid options for exploring the wider city by public transit.

One important note on parking: it's valet-only, running about 38 dollars per night, which does include in-and-out privileges. There's also an electric car charging station on-site, a nice bonus for EV drivers.

POLICIES AND PRACTICAL DETAILS

Check-in starts at 4 PM with flexible late arrival allowed, and check-out is at 11 AM. This property has a minimum check-in age of 21, so keep that in mind if you're booking for a group of younger travelers without an adult on the reservation — families are still welcome, and children can absolutely stay here; the age rule applies specifically to who checks in at the front desk.

You'll want a government-issued photo ID and a credit card, debit card, or cash deposit ready at check-in to cover incidental charges — fairly standard, but worth having ready.

One detail that stood out in the reviews: this is a genuinely pet-friendly hotel. One guest traveling with two small dogs specifically noted that the policy wasn't just something listed on a website — it was honored in practice, with staff going out of their way to make the stay comfortable for their pets too.

WHAT GUESTS SAY, THE HONEST VERSION

No property is flawless, so here's the honest picture rather than just the highlight reel. The most common piece of constructive feedback was about service speed during busy periods — a few guests mentioned things slowing down at capacity, and one review specifically mentioned a staff member who came across as short with guests.

But even the guest who raised that criticism was on their third stay, and closed their review already planning a fourth visit. Another minor note: one traveler mentioned wishing their room had a full-sized mirror — a small, easily fixed complaint in an otherwise glowing review.

Compare that to the sheer volume of five-star praise across nearly two thousand reviews, and these read as minor footnotes rather than real red flags.

WHO SHOULD BOOK THIS HOTEL

So who is this actually built for? If you want a boutique, design-forward property with real history behind it, a strong pool experience, genuinely helpful staff, and food worth planning a meal around, this checks every box. It works well for couples celebrating something — birthdays come up repeatedly in reviews — and just as well for families, thanks to the Family Suite option and a pet-friendly policy backed by real guest experiences, not just marketing language.

If your priority is walking distance to major attractions like Balboa Park, easy beach access within a short drive, and a strong on-site experience so you're not planning every hour of your trip, the Lafayette hits that balance well.


To sum it up: the Lafayette Hotel and Club is a 9.4-rated, four-star historic property in San Diego, with a Family Suite that sleeps six starting around 169 dollars a night, two on-site restaurants, a genuinely photogenic pool courtyard, a full spa and gym, easy beach access, and a pet-friendly reputation backed by real guest stories.

If you're planning a trip to San Diego and want a property that feels like part of the destination rather than just a place to sleep, this one deserves a serious look.
